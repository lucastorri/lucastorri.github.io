Personal Mail Archive

Personal Mail Archive keeps a local, read-only copy of selected email from its user's own Gmail account.

What it does

  • Reads messages under Gmail labels the user has chosen, with read-only access.
  • Saves those messages and their attachments as files on the user's own devices, so they can be searched, read and organised there.
  • Never sends, changes or deletes email.

Who it is for

It is a single-user tool, run by its author for his own Gmail account. It is not distributed and there is nothing to install or sign up for here.
